Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Heather Games ("we", "us"), available at heathergame.com, handles information when you visit the site and play our browser games.

Information we collect

Our games run in your browser and do not require an account. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address or other personal details to play. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type and pages visited, for security and to keep the site working.

Cookies and local storage

Some games may store settings or progress (for example scores or sound preferences) in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements. Google and its partners, including third-party vendors, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Some games may offer optional rewarded ads, where you can choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward such as extra ammo or an extra life. Watching these ads is always optional.

Visitors from the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you visit from these regions, you may see a consent message that lets you choose whether to allow cookies and personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time using the options shown on the site.

Children's privacy

Our site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party links

Our site may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact
