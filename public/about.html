About Heather Games

Heather Games is a small collection of free browser games that you can play on your phone or computer, with nothing to install and no account needed.

Our games

  • The Lost Treasure - a 3D adventure where you hunt for four ancient map pieces across jungle, desert, snow and volcano, survive wild animals and open the legendary chest.
  • Ten Circles - a rhythm dance battle in ten styles with 3D dancers and a rival.
  • Tessera - a block puzzle: drag blocks onto the board and clear lines.
  • Heather GP - a racing game: race three laps and dodge the traffic.
  • Banh Dua - a chopsticks skill game.

Our goal

We want to make fun, polished games that start instantly and work everywhere. The site is free to play and supported by advertising.

New games coming regularly

We are always building. New and interesting games are added regularly, so check back often to find something new to play.
