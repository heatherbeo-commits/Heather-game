    How to play

    Arrows fall down four lanes. Hit each one as it reaches the glowing pad, using ← ↓ ↑ → or D F J K. On a phone, tap the lanes. Perfect hits and long combos score more, and a miss makes your dancer stumble. Win two of three rounds to beat the rival. Press Esc to pause, or 1 to 9 and 0 to start a style from the menu.
