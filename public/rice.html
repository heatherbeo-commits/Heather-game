🌾 Tam's Rice Challenge

A Vietnamese folk-tale puzzle · Tấm Cám

Story

Once upon a time, Tam was given an impossible task: separate a huge pile of rice and paddy before sunset.

The rice grains and paddy grains were mixed together. Just when Tam was about to give up, a flock of little sparrows arrived to help.

Now it's your turn to help Tam!

How to play

  • Find and collect the white rice grains ⚪ → left basket.
  • Separate them from the yellow paddy grains 🟡 → right basket.
  • Drag a grain into its basket, or tap the grain then tap the basket.
  • Complete each challenge before the timer runs out.
  • Earn stars and unlock new levels.

Special features

  • Golden Rice: bonus points for finding rare golden grains (put them in the rice basket).
  • Sparrow Power: birds help collect several grains at once.
  • Perfect Sorting: extra star for making no mistakes.
  • Time Bonus: finish early to earn additional rewards.
